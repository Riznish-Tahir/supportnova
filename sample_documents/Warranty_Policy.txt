NovaCart Warranty Policy
Document ID: WAR-POL-09 | Version: 1.2 | Effective: 2026-02-01

1. Standard Coverage
1.1 All NovaCart-branded electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from date of purchase.
1.2 Premium Care subscribers receive an extended 24-month warranty.

2. Claims
2.1 Warranty claims require proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt).
2.2 Claims for units past the warranty window are not eligible unless Premium Care applies.
