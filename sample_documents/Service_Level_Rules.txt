NovaCart Service Level Agreement Rules
Document ID: SLA-POL-18 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

Priority P0 (Critical): first response within 1 hour, resolution target 24 hours.
Priority P1 (High): first response within 4 hours, resolution target 48 hours.
Priority P2 (Medium): first response within 24 hours, resolution target 5 business days.
Priority P3 (Low): first response within 48 hours, resolution target 10 business days.
