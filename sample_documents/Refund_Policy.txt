NovaCart Refund Policy
Document ID: REF-POL-03 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 2026-06-15

1. Scope
This policy governs refunds for all NovaCart e-commerce purchases.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Items may be refunded within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
2.2 Digital subscriptions are non-refundable after 7 days of renewal.
2.3 Items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for refund.

3. Partial Refunds
3.1 Items returned without original accessories may receive a partial refund at 80% of value.

4. Processing Time
4.1 Approved refunds are issued within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

5. Unsupported Requests
5.1 Refund requests outside the 30-day window require Department Manager approval.
5.2 No refund shall be promised or guaranteed by automated systems without policy verification.
