NovaCart Service Quality Policy
Document ID: SVC-POL-08 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

1. Service quality complaints include slow responses, repeated transfers, unhelpful communication, and unresolved service interactions.
2. Agents must review the customer's prior support history before closing a repeated service complaint.
3. Customers should receive clear, professional, and accurate communication throughout the resolution process.
4. Service complaints must not be dismissed without reviewing the underlying issue.
5. Serious or repeated service failures may require supervisor or management review.
