NovaCart Escalation Procedure
Document ID: ESC-POL-13 | Version: 2.3 | Effective: 2026-05-11

1. Mandatory Escalation Triggers
1.1 Any Safety category complaint (fire, shock, injury) — escalate immediately to Critical Management Escalation.
1.2 Any confirmed data breach or unauthorized account access — escalate to Compliance Review.
1.3 Repeat unresolved complaints (same customer, same issue, prior status = unresolved) — escalate to Department Manager.
1.4 Complaints containing legal-threat language ("lawsuit", "regulator", "lawyer") — escalate to Compliance Review.

2. Escalation Levels
No Escalation < Supervisor Review < Department Manager < Specialist Team < Compliance Review < Critical Management Escalation.

3. Urgency vs. Sentiment
3.1 Urgency must be determined by objective risk factors, not by the emotional tone of the complaint text.
