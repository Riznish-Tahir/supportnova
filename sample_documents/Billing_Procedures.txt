3. Duplicate and Incorrect Charges
3.1 Duplicate charges must be verified against the payment gateway transaction log before refund.
3.2 Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded within 5-7 business days.
3.3 A second duplicate-billing incident on the same account within 90 days triggers mandatory escalation
    to the Billing Manager (see Escalation Procedure ESC-POL-13).

4. Subscription Renewals
4.1 Customers must be notified by email at least 3 days before automatic renewal.
