NovaCart Returns Policy
Document ID: RET-POL-01 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-01

1. Damaged, defective, incomplete, or incorrect items may be eligible for return after verification.
2. Customers may be asked to provide order details, photos, or other evidence before approval.
3. Returns must follow the approved eligibility window and product-condition requirements.
4. Refund, replacement, or compensation must not be promised before policy eligibility is verified.
5. Missing parts and wrong-item cases must be checked against fulfillment and order records.
