NovaCart Privacy Policy
Document ID: PRI-POL-10 | Version: 1.0 | Effective: 2026-01-15

1. Data We Collect
Order history, contact details, and support interactions.

2. Customer Rights
2.1 Customers may request a copy of their data (Data Access Request) within 30 days.
2.2 Marketing opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days of request.

3. Breach Notification
3.1 Any suspected data breach must be routed to Compliance Review immediately, regardless of tone.
