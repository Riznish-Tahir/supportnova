NovaCart Complaint Handling SOP
Document ID: SOP-POL-15 | Version: 3.2 | Effective: 2026-08-01

1. Intake
1.1 All complaints must be logged with a unique Complaint ID within 5 minutes of submission.

2. Analysis
2.1 Every complaint is analyzed by the GenAI Complaint Intelligence Pipeline and independently verified
    by the Python Ground-Truth Validation Pipeline before a response is sent to the customer.

3. Response
3.1 Generated responses must acknowledge the issue, avoid unsupported promises, and reference only
    verified policy sections.
